Learn Recommendation Systems with Phoebe

How "recommended for you" actually works

The engine behind Netflix's homepage, Amazon's "customers who bought this also bought", and Lazada's feed - explained end to end. A leader track that makes every type click in plain English (with a visual for each), and a builder track that implements them in real Python: basket analysis, customer-to-customer and item-to-item collaborative filtering, matrix factorization, and modern deep models. You even drive a live recommender in the browser. And the whole team pipeline - analyst to data scientist to engineering to monitoring - laid out so you know who does what.

An honest note on the examples: Netflix, Amazon, and Alibaba run production stacks far larger than any 45-minute session. This course teaches the published, named core each is famous for - Amazon's item-to-item CF (2003), matrix factorization from the Netflix Prize, and Alibaba's Deep Interest Network - and cites the real papers. The live recommender is real math on a tiny teaching catalog, not a production engine.
